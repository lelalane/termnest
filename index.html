        About us

        Made by UM students, for UM students

        If you’ve ever tried to sublet at UM, you know how it goes. You post in a Facebook group or a giant group chat and scroll through a hundred messages. Half the rooms are already gone, the dates never quite match your semester, and someone always wants a deposit over Zelle before you’ve even seen the place.

        That’s where students get scammed by listings that aren’t real. It’s where people sign a sublease and only later find out their landlord never said yes. And honestly, most of us had no idea whether we were even allowed to sublet in the first place.

        So we built TermNest. We’re UM students, and we wanted a safe, simple way to sublet that actually fits the school calendar: spring, summer, fall and full-year terms, with real move-in and move-out dates. Everyone on it is a verified UM student, your rent is held until move-in day, and getting your landlord’s OK is part of the process, not an afterthought.

        • ✕Fake listings and deposits sent to strangers
        • ✕Dates that don’t line up with the semester
        • ✕No one knows if their lease even allows subletting

          Safety

          How TermNest keeps you safe

          Subletting means trusting someone you just met. TermNest is built so you don’t have to take anyone’s word for it.

          Rent is held until move-in

          You never send rent straight to a stranger. Our third-party payment processor holds your first month’s rent until move-in day, when you’re in the room and happy with it.

          1. ReservedYou pay the first month in the app. Our payment processor holds it until move-in day.
          2. Lease signedYou and the host sign the sublease in the app. The host can see the rent is paid, but can’t take it out yet.
          3. Moved inOn move-in day, you see the room in person and take your move-in photos.
          4. Payment releasedAfter you confirm move-in, the payment processor pays the host.

          Room isn’t as listed? Don’t confirm move-in. Report it in the app instead. Your rent stays held while support looks into it, and if the room isn’t as listed, you get a full refund.

          Every student is verified

          You can’t post, message or book on TermNest until you prove you’re a student.

          How verification works

          • You sign up with your school email (ending in .edu).
          • We send a 6-digit code to that inbox.
          • You enter the code to finish signing up. Only someone who can open that school inbox can do this.

          What you’ll see in the app

          Verified student

          This badge is on every host and every chat. TermNest’s own support team shows an Official account badge, so you always know when it’s really us.

          Move-in photo check

          On move-in day, the app asks you to photograph the room before you confirm. It protects both students.

          What to photograph

          • Each room you’ll use, from the doorway
          • Furniture, appliances and anything already damaged
          • Walls, floors, windows and the bathroom

          Why it matters

          • Photos are saved with your booking and dated.
          • If the room doesn’t match the listing, they back up your report.
          • Hosts can see the room’s condition when you arrived.

          Sublet scam warning signs

          Scammers target students looking for short-term housing, especially before a new semester. Watch for these signs, on TermNest or anywhere else.

          • !They want payment outside the appZelle, Venmo, Cash App, cash, a wire transfer or gift cards. Money sent this way usually can’t be gotten back.
          • !The price is too good to be trueRent far below what similar rooms cost. Check the fair price tag on the listing.
          • !You can’t see the roomThey won’t do a video tour or let a friend visit, often because they say they’re “out of the country.”
          • !They rush you“Lots of people want it, pay today to hold it.” Real hosts give you time to decide.
          • !They want to leave the appThey ask to move to text, WhatsApp or personal email before you’ve booked.
          • !They skip the paperworkNo landlord approval, no sublease agreement, or “we don’t need to sign anything.”
          • !They ask for private informationYour Social Security number, bank login or school password. TermNest will never ask for these.
          • !The details don’t matchPhotos that look like a hotel or ad, an address that changes, or dates that keep shifting.

          If something feels off

          1. Stop. Don’t send any money.
          2. Report the listing or chat in the app.
          3. Contact TermNest support. A team member replies within 1 hour in the app.

          Frequently asked questions

          Who can use TermNest?

          Students with a .edu school email. TermNest is built for University of Miami students, and Term Swap students from other schools verify the same way. You confirm a code sent to that email before you can post or message. We verify the email only and don’t do background checks.

          How does “rent held until move-in” work?

          When you reserve, you pay the first month in the app. Our third-party payment processor holds it until move-in day. After you move in and confirm the room is as listed, the money goes to the host. If the room isn’t as listed, you get a full refund.

          When do I pay the $15 success fee?

          Only after a subletter signs the sublease agreement for your room. Posting is free, and if nobody signs, you pay nothing.

          What does the $5 Boost do?

          Boost puts your listing at the top of search results and adds a Boost tag. It’s optional, costs $5, is charged upfront when you turn it on, and isn’t refundable.

          Do I need my landlord’s permission?

          Most leases require it. TermNest lets you send a request to your landlord from the app and adds their approval to the sublease agreement.

          What is Term Swap?

          If you’re leaving Miami for a semester, Term Swap finds a student coming to Miami from the place you’re going, so you can trade rooms. You each keep paying your own rent, so no money changes hands. Try a Term Swap search.

          Do I have to fill out roommate preferences?

          No. Preferences are optional. If you add them, you’ll see a match score on rooms that have roommates.

          What if something is wrong on move-in day?

          Take move-in photos in the app and contact support. Your rent stays held while the team sorts it out.

          This policy explains what information TermNest collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It works together with our Terms of Use.

          1. The short version

          • We collect what we need to run TermNest and keep students safe.
          • We never sell your data.
          • We only share it with storage, moving, furniture rental, or insurance partners if you choose one of their perks.
          • You can delete your account and your data at any time.

          2. What we collect

          • Your name and .edu email, to create your account and confirm that you’re a student.
          • Your profile, like your school, year, and anything you choose to add about yourself.
          • Roommate preferences, like your sleep schedule, cleanliness, and guests, if you choose to answer them.
          • Messages you send and receive in TermNest.
          • Listing details and photos you post.
          • Payment information, like your card. This is collected and stored by a third-party payment processor, not by TermNest. We only see basic details, like the last 4 digits of your card, so you can tell your cards apart.

          3. How we use it

          • To create your account and verify that you’re a student.
          • To show your listings and profile to other verified students.
          • To suggest roommate matches based on your preferences.
          • To deliver your messages and booking updates.
          • To charge fees and hold rent until move-in, through our payment processor.
          • To keep TermNest safe, for example by finding fake listings and scams.
          • To answer your questions and improve TermNest.

          4. We never sell your data

          TermNest does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Not to advertisers, not to landlords, not to anyone.

          5. When we share it

          We only share your information in these cases:

          • With other students, when you use TermNest. Students can see your listings, your public profile, and the messages you send them. Your email and phone number are not shown.
          • With our payment processor, so it can charge fees and hold rent.
          • With storage, moving, furniture rental, and insurance partners, only if you choose one of their perks. We share just what they need to help you, like your name, email, move dates, and the address of the room for renters insurance. If you don’t choose a perk, they get nothing.
          • If the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

          6. Keeping it safe

          We use reasonable security measures to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your school email secure and never share your verification codes.

          7. Deleting your account and data

          You can ask us to delete your account and your data at any time:

          • In the app, go to Profile → Help & safety → Chat with support and ask to delete your account.
          • Or email hello@termnest.app from your school email.

          We’ll delete your account, profile, preferences, messages, and listings within 30 days. We may keep a small amount of information when the law requires it, like records of payments, or to finish an open sublease or fee.

          8. Students only, 18 and older

          TermNest is only for students who are 18 or older. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If we learn that we have, we’ll delete it.

          9. Changes to this policy

          If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change is important, we’ll tell you in the app or by email first.
