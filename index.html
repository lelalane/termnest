      Safety

      How TermNest keeps you safe

      Subletting means trusting someone you just met. TermNest is built so you don’t have to take anyone’s word for it.

      Rent is held until move-in

      You never send rent straight to a stranger. Your first month’s rent waits with TermNest until you’re in the room and happy with it.

      1. You pay in the appWhen you reserve, you pay the first month inside TermNest.
      2. TermNest holds itThe host can see it’s paid, but can’t take it out yet.
      3. You move in and checkYou see the room in person and take your move-in photos.
      4. Rent is releasedAfter you confirm move-in, the host gets paid.

      Room isn’t as listed? Don’t confirm move-in. Report it in the app instead. Your rent stays held while support looks into it, and if the room isn’t as listed, you get a full refund.

      Every student is verified

      You can’t post, message or book on TermNest until you prove you’re a student.

      How verification works

      • You sign up with your school email (ending in .edu).
      • We send a 6-digit code to that inbox.
      • You enter the code to finish signing up. Only someone who can open that school inbox can do this.

      What you’ll see in the app

      Verified student

      This badge is on every host and every chat. TermNest’s own support team shows an Official account badge, so you always know when it’s really us.

      Move-in photo check

      On move-in day, the app asks you to photograph the room before you confirm. It protects both students.

      What to photograph

      • Each room you’ll use, from the doorway
      • Furniture, appliances and anything already damaged
      • Walls, floors, windows and the bathroom

      Why it matters

      • Photos are saved with your booking and dated.
      • If the room doesn’t match the listing, they back up your report.
      • Hosts can see the room’s condition when you arrived.

      Sublet scam warning signs

      Scammers target students looking for short-term housing, especially before a new semester. Watch for these signs, on TermNest or anywhere else.

      • !They want payment outside the appZelle, Venmo, Cash App, cash, a wire transfer or gift cards. Money sent this way usually can’t be gotten back.
      • !The price is too good to be trueRent far below what similar rooms cost. Check the fair price tag on the listing.
      • !You can’t see the roomThey won’t do a video tour or let a friend visit, often because they say they’re “out of the country.”
      • !They rush you“Lots of people want it, pay today to hold it.” Real hosts give you time to decide.
      • !They want to leave the appThey ask to move to text, WhatsApp or personal email before you’ve booked.
      • !They skip the paperworkNo landlord approval, no sublease agreement, or “we don’t need to sign anything.”
      • !They ask for private informationYour Social Security number, bank login or school password. TermNest will never ask for these.
      • !The details don’t matchPhotos that look like a hotel or ad, an address that changes, or dates that keep shifting.

      If something feels off

      1. Stop. Don’t send any money.
      2. Report the listing or chat in the app.
      3. Contact TermNest support. A team member replies within 1 hour in the app.

      Frequently asked questions

      Who can use TermNest?

      University of Miami students with a school email. You confirm a code sent to that email before you can post or message.

      How does “rent held until move-in” work?

      When you reserve, you pay the first month in the app. TermNest holds it. After you move in and confirm the room is as listed, the money goes to the host. If the room isn’t as listed, you get a full refund.

      When do I pay the $15 listing fee?

      Only after a subletter signs the sublease agreement for your room. Posting is free, and if nobody signs, you pay nothing.

      What does the $5 Boost do?

      Boost moves your listing higher in search results and adds a Boost tag. It’s optional and charged when you turn it on.

      Do I need my landlord’s permission?

      Most leases require it. TermNest lets you send a request to your landlord from the app and adds their approval to the sublease agreement.

      What is Term Swap?

      If you’re leaving Miami for a semester, Term Swap finds a student coming to Miami from the place you’re going, so you can trade rooms.

      Do I have to fill out roommate preferences?

      No. Preferences are optional. If you add them, you’ll see a match score on rooms that have roommates.

      What if something is wrong on move-in day?

      Take move-in photos in the app and contact support. Your rent stays held while the team sorts it out.
